Upload a monthly sales-by-customer Excel export and get a management-ready review deck with charts, ready to download as PDF, PowerPoint or Excel. Files are read in your browser and are not uploaded anywhere.

1 · Upload the Excel

Drop the .xlsx file here Or choose it from your device. One sheet, customer rows followed by their product rows.
Expected file format
  • Row 1: Particular, then one merged header per month (e.g. August 2026) and a final Total.
  • Row 2: Amount / Quantity under each month.
  • A customer name row with no numbers, followed by that customer's product rows.
  • Product rows start with an item code in brackets: [EM-2313] Disposable Syringe 3ml - Euromed.
  • Negative values are treated as returns or credit notes. A customer containing “Cash” is treated as walk-in sales.
